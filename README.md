# HarvestNews V1

HarvestNews is an AI-assisted news and community platform foundation.

## Included
- Responsive homepage
- News categories
- News cards / newsroom placeholder
- Forum landing area
- About, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Editorial Policy and Community Rules pages
- Signup placeholder
- AdSense-ready site structure

## Next integrations
1. Connect a real database/authentication service.
2. Connect permitted news feeds/APIs.
3. Add the AI processing pipeline.
4. Add source attribution and duplicate/spam safeguards.
5. Add admin moderation dashboard.
6. Add analytics and advertising after the site has real content and traffic.
7. Replace placeholder legal text and contact email with the site's real information.

## Important
Do not use this starter to automatically copy and republish third-party articles. Production news ingestion must respect each source's licensing and terms.
