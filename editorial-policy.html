Editorial Policy

HarvestNews aims to present useful, current information with clear sourcing. Automated tools may assist with discovery, summarization, classification and drafting.

Sources and attribution

We intend to use permitted feeds, APIs, public information and other sources according to their terms. Source attribution should be provided where appropriate.

Corrections

Material errors should be corrected promptly. Readers can report concerns to the administration team.

AI use

AI output is treated as an editorial aid, not as a guarantee of truth. Automated safeguards and human review may be required for sensitive or high-impact stories.
